QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before your adventure.

In development · iPhone / iPad

Which devices can play?+

You need an iPhone or iPad with iOS or iPadOS 27 or later, Apple Intelligence enabled, and a model that supports image input.

Can I download it now?+

Drawmon is in development and preparing for release. An App Store release date will be announced when it is ready.

Do I have to be good at drawing?+

This is not a drawing contest. Shapes, tools and materials help communicate your idea. Look at the opponent and experiment.

Where are drawings and progress saved?+

The app saves your drawings and adventure on your device. There is autosave and three manual bookmarks. Ads and purchases use an internet connection.

Which languages are supported?+

This website is available in 16 language and script settings. The app's normal language choices are Japanese and English. Fourteen additional settings are being developed as previews.

How do redrawing and summoning work?+

Creating a drawing and redrawing are free. Watch an ad to summon a saved companion, then retry that floor together. A one-time purchase removes ads.
